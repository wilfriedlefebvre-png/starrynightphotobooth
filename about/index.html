About Us

Creating Memories That Last Beyond the Event

Based in Orange County, Starry Night Photo Booths brings your celebration to life with more than just photos—we create moments of joy that guests relive for years to come. Whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand occasion, our team combines creativity, technology, and personalized service to make your event truly memorable.

Since 2018, we've been serving Orange County communities with a commitment to excellence that goes beyond simply providing equipment. We believe every celebration deserves an experience tailored to its unique character. That's why we invest time in understanding your vision, collaborating on custom designs, and ensuring every detail reflects your personal style.

From Newport Beach weddings to Irvine corporate events and private celebrations throughout Aliso Viejo, Costa Mesa, and beyond, we've had the privilege of being part of countless special moments. Our clients consistently praise our attention to detail, professional service, and the genuine care we bring to every event. When you choose Starry Night Photo Booths, you're not just hiring a vendor—you're partnering with a team that treats your celebration as our own.

  • Studio-Quality Results

    Using premium equipment, refined lighting techniques, and beautifully designed prints, we ensure every image looks sharp and polished. Our booth setups are thoughtfully arranged to complement your event space while delivering consistently excellent photos. We don't believe in shortcuts—our professional-grade cameras, studio lighting, and high-definition printing produce results that rival professional photography studios.

  • Collaborative Approach

    We take time to understand your celebration's unique style and atmosphere. Our attentive team partners with you throughout the process—designing custom elements, coordinating details, and ensuring everything reflects your personal vision from start to finish. Communication is key to our success, and we maintain responsive, clear dialogue from your initial inquiry through final photo delivery.

  • Complete photo booth experiences with professional setup, attentive operation, and thorough cleanup.
  • Specialization in custom design, event coordination, and guest engagement across all event types.
  • Military and first responder discounts available as our way of giving back to the community.
