Luxury Wedding Photo Booth Options

Glam Booth Experience features celebrity-style black and white photography with professional lighting along with smoothing filters. Give your guests the gift of stunning, frame-worthy portraits that are simply timeless.

Personalized Photo Prints

360° Video Booth Experience

Delivers show-stopping slow-motion videos that guests will share instantly on social media. Watch your reception come alive with interactive moments captured from every angle.
