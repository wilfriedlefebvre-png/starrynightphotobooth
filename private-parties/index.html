Orange County Photo Booth Rentals for Every Private Celebration

Whether you're planning a birthday bash, graduation party, anniversary celebration, bar or bat mitzvah, or retirement party, our photo booth rentals bring energy and entertainment to your event. We serve communities throughout Orange County, including Aliso Viejo, Newport Beach, Irvine, RSM, Lake Forest, Costa Mesa, and beyond. Each private party is unique, and we pride ourselves on creating a customized experience that reflects your celebration's personality and style.

Our open-air photo booths accommodate groups of all sizes, from solo shots to large family photos. With unlimited prints, custom props, and backdrops tailored to your party theme, your guests can capture spontaneous, joyful moments throughout your event. The beauty of a photo booth at a private party is simple: it brings people together, breaks the ice, and creates instant keepsakes that everyone can take home.

Custom Design That Brings Your Vision to Life

What makes Starry Night Photo Booths different is our in-house graphic design expertise. We don't rely on generic templates. Instead, we work closely with you to create custom print designs that match your party theme, colors, and style. From elegant milestone anniversaries to vibrant celebrations, every detail is personalized to make your event truly special.

Our design consultation process ensures your photo booth perfectly complements your celebration. Want your graduate's school colors and future college logo on the prints? Planning a 50th birthday with a specific theme? Celebrating a retirement with company memories? We collaborate with you to bring these ideas to life, creating prints your guests will actually want to keep and share.

Orange County Photo Booth Services Built for Stress-Free Celebrations

Planning a private party should be exciting, not stressful. That's why we handle every detail from start to finish. Our team arrives early for quiet, efficient setup, ensuring everything is ready before your first guest arrives. Our friendly, professional attendants stay throughout your event, helping guests capture the perfect shots and keeping the photo booth running smoothly.

For hosts seeking a more budget-friendly option, we also offer a drop-off and pickup service. This DIY-style rental includes everything you need, backdrop, props, and lighting, allowing you to run the photo booth yourself while keeping costs down. It's perfect for smaller gatherings where you want the fun of a photo booth without the full-service investment.

Technology That Enhances the Experience

Our photo booths blend professional quality with modern convenience. Studio lighting and high-definition cameras ensure every photo looks crisp and vibrant. Guests can instantly share their pictures via social media, text, or email, extending the joy of your celebration beyond the event itself. Within 23 to 48 hours after your party, you'll receive access to a private digital gallery containing every photo captured, perfect for reliving the memories and sharing with guests who couldn't attend.

One of our most popular features is the slideshow monitor, which displays photos in real-time during your event. As guests take pictures, they appear on a screen for everyone to enjoy, creating a dynamic, interactive element that keeps the energy high throughout your celebration. It's entertainment that builds on itself, encouraging more guests to jump in and have fun.

Similar to our wedding photo booth services, we offer GIF and boomerang capabilities, 360-degree video booth options for show-stopping moments, and customizable backgrounds using green screen technology. These features can elevate any private party from memorable to truly extraordinary.
